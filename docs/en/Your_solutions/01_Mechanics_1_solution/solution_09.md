# Physics Problem: Momentum Comparison

This document provides a step-by-step comparison of the momentum between two objects with different masses and velocities.

---

## 1. Definitions and Formulas

### Linear Momentum
**Momentum** ($p$) is a vector quantity that measures the "amount of motion" an object has. It is the product of an object's mass and its velocity.

The standard formula is:

$$
p = m \cdot v
$$

Where:
* **$p$**: Momentum (measured in $kg \cdot m/s$)
* **$m$**: Mass (measured in $kg$)
* **$v$**: Velocity (measured in $m/s$)

### Unit Conversions
To compare momentum accurately, all units must be consistent (SI units). Since the masses are given in grams, we convert them to kilograms:

$$
1 \text{ g} = 0.001 \text{ kg}
$$

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Calculation

### Step 1: Calculate the momentum of the fly
First, we identify the knowns for the fly and convert the mass to kilograms.

* **Mass ($m_{fly}$):** $2 \text{ g} = 0.002 \text{ kg}$
* **Velocity ($v_{fly}$):** $10 \text{ m/s}$

Applying the formula:

$$
p_{fly} = 0.002 \text{ kg} \cdot 10 \text{ m/s}
$$

$$
p_{fly} = 0.02 \text{ kg} \cdot m/s
$$

### Step 2: Calculate the momentum of the tennis ball
Next, we identify the knowns for the tennis ball and convert the mass to kilograms.

* **Mass ($m_{ball}$):** $60 \text{ g} = 0.06 \text{ kg}$
* **Velocity ($v_{ball}$):** $1 \text{ m/s}$

Applying the formula:

$$
p_{ball} = 0.06 \text{ kg} \cdot 1 \text{ m/s}
$$

$$
p_{ball} = 0.06 \text{ kg} \cdot m/s
$$

---

## 3. Comparison and Conclusion

Comparing the two results:

* **Fly:** $0.02 \text{ kg} \cdot m/s$
* **Tennis Ball:** $0.06 \text{ kg} \cdot m/s$

Since $0.06 > 0.02$, we conclude:

$$
p_{ball} > p_{fly}
$$

**Final Answer:** The **60-gram tennis ball** moving at $1$ m/s has greater momentum than the 2-gram fly flying at $10$ m/s. Even though the fly is $10$ times faster, the tennis ball is $30$ times more massive, which results in higher momentum.
