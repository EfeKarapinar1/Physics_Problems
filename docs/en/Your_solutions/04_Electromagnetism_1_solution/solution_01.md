# Electrostatics Problem: Force on a Central Charge

## Problem Statement
Four point charges of +1.0 C each are placed at the corners of a square with sides of 1.0 m. Calculate the magnitude and direction of the electric force on a charge of -2.0 C placed at the center of the square.

## 1. Relevant Formulas
The magnitude of the electrostatic force is given by Coulomb's Law:

$$
F = k \frac{|q_1 q_2|}{r^2}
$$

The distance from the corner to the center of a square with side $a$:

$$
r = \frac{a\sqrt{2}}{2}
$$

## 2. Solution Steps

### Step 1: Geometry
Side $a = 1.0$ m.
Distance $r$:

$$
r = \frac{1.0\sqrt{2}}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \text{ m}
$$

### Step 2: Individual Force Magnitude
The force from one corner charge on the center charge:

$$
F = k \frac{|(1.0)(-2.0)|}{(\sqrt{2}/2)^2} = k \frac{2}{0.5} = 4k
$$

### Step 3: Vector Sum (Symmetry)
Because the charges at the corners are identical and the center charge is at the geometric center:
- The force from the top-left corner is canceled by the force from the bottom-right corner.
- The force from the top-right corner is canceled by the force from the bottom-left corner.

## 3. Final Answer
The net electric force is:

$$
\vec{F}_{net} = 0 \text{ N}
$$

The magnitude is **0 N** and there is **no direction**.
