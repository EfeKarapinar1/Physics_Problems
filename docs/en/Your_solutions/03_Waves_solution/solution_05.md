# Physics Problem: Echo Ranging

## Problem

A person shouts towards a cliff and hears the echo 1 seconds later. How far away is the cliff? (Speed of sound in air is 343 m/s).

## 1. Definitions and Formulas

- **Echo**: A reflection of sound that reaches the listener with a delay after the direct sound.
- **Speed of Sound ($v$)**: The distance sound travels per unit of time.
- **Echo Distance Formula**: Since sound travels to the barrier and back, the distance ($d$) is half of the total distance covered.

$$
d = \frac{v \times t}{2}
$$

---

## 2. Problem Statement

A person shouts towards a cliff and hears the echo 1 second later. How far away is the cliff? (Speed of sound in air is 343 m/s).

### Given:
- $v = 343 \, \text{m/s}$
- $t = 1 \, \text{s}$

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1: Calculate total distance traveled by sound
First, we find the total distance the sound wave traveled (to the cliff and back) using the formula $s = v \times t$.

$$
s = 343 \, \text{m/s} \times 1 \, \text{s} = 343 \, \text{m}
$$

### Step 2: Divide by 2 to find the distance to the cliff
Since the sound traveled to the cliff and returned, the actual distance to the cliff is exactly half of the total distance.

$$
d = \frac{343 \, \text{m}}{2}
$$

### Step 3: Final Result
$$
d = 171.5 \, \text{m}
$$

**Answer:** The cliff is **171.5 meters** away.
