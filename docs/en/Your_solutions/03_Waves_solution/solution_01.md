# Physics Problem: Wave Properties

## Question

A sound wave in air has a frequency of 440 Hz. If the speed of sound in air is 343 m/s, what is its wavelength? What is its wavelength in water, where the speed of sound is 1482 m/s?

## 1. Necessary Definitions and Formulas

- **Frequency ($f$):** Number of cycles per second ($Hz$).
- **Wavelength ($\lambda$):** Distance between consecutive wave peaks ($m$).
- **Wave Speed ($v$):** Speed of the wave in a medium ($m/s$).

The fundamental relationship is:

$$
v = f \cdot \lambda
$$

Rearranging for wavelength:

$$
\lambda = \frac{v}{f}
$$

---

## 2. Problem Solution

**Problem:** A sound wave has a frequency of $440 \text{ Hz}$. Find its wavelength in air ($v = 343 \text{ m/s}$) and in water ($v = 1482 \text{ m/s}$).

### Step 1: Wavelength in Air

Using the known speed of sound in air:

$$
\lambda_{\text{air}} = \frac{343}{440}
$$

$$
\lambda_{\text{air}} \approx 0.780 \text{ m}
$$

### Step 2: Wavelength in Water

Using the speed of sound in water and keeping frequency constant:

$$
\lambda_{\text{water}} = \frac{1482}{440}
$$

$$
\lambda_{\text{water}} \approx 3.37 \text{ m}
$$

---

## 3. Summary of Results

| Medium | Speed ($v$) | Frequency ($f$) | Wavelength ($\lambda$) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Air | $343 \text{ m/s}$ | $440 \text{ Hz}$ | $\approx 0.78 \text{ m}$ |
| Water | $1482 \text{ m/s}$ | $440 \text{ Hz}$ | $\approx 3.37 \text{ m}$ |
