# Wave Equation Analysis

## Problem

A wave is described by the equation $y(x,t) = 0.05 \sin(2\pi x - 50\pi t)$, where x and y are in meters and t is in seconds. Determine the waves':

a) Amplitude $A$.

b) Wavelength $\lambda$.

c) Frequency $f$.

d) Wave speed $v$.

## Problem Statement
A wave is described by the equation:

$$
y(x,t) = 0.05 \sin(2\pi x - 50\pi t)
$$

Where $x$ and $y$ are in meters and $t$ is in seconds.

---

## 1. Constants and Formulas
To solve this, we compare the given equation to the standard form:

$$
y(x,t) = A \sin(kx - \omega t)
$$

### Formulas used:
- **Wave Number:** $k = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda}$
- **Angular Frequency:** $\omega = 2\pi f$
- **Wave Speed:** $v = f \lambda$

---

## 2. Calculations

### a) Amplitude ($A$)
By direct comparison with the equation:
**$A = 0.05 \text{ m}$**

### b) Wavelength ($\lambda$)
Given $k = 2\pi$:

$$
\lambda = \frac{2\pi}{k} = \frac{2\pi}{2\pi} = 1 \text{ m}
$$

**$\lambda = 1 \text{ m}$**

### c) Frequency ($f$)
Given $\omega = 50\pi$:

$$
f = \frac{\omega}{2\pi} = \frac{50\pi}{2\pi} = 25 \text{ Hz}
$$

**$f = 25 \text{ Hz}$**

### d) Wave Speed ($v$)
Using the results from (b) and (c):

$$
v = f \cdot \lambda = 25 \cdot 1 = 25 \text{ m/s}
$$

**$v = 25 \text{ m/s}$**
